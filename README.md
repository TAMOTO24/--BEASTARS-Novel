## 📖 About the Project

**BEASTARS-Novel** is set in a dynamic and complex world of **anthropomorphic animals**, inspired by the universe of the *Beastars* series.  
This novella explores deep **social, emotional, and psychological themes**, focusing on the intricate relationships between different species within a society marked by **tension, prejudice, and the search for personal identity**.

In this story, characters navigate the struggles of coexistence in a world where carnivores and herbivores must live side by side.  
With rich character development, moral dilemmas, and intense societal dynamics, this novella delves into themes of **power, vulnerability**, and the **universal quest for self-understanding**.

Whether you're a fan of the original *Beastars* series or simply enjoy **thought-provoking stories** about society and identity,  
**BEASTARS-Novel** offers a fresh perspective on a world where **animal instincts and human emotions collide.**
